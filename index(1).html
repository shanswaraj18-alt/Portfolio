Skill Munch

A platform concept connecting people with side skills—such as singing, dancing, stand-up, photography, and DJing—to event organizers looking to hire performers.

Performers can create profiles with skills, short videos, availability, and expected pay. Organizers can post events and find suitable acts. Features include radius-based “Skill Munch Local” search, verified profiles, reviews, and portfolio videos to help build trust.
